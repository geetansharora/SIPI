Signal and power integrity calculators

Twelve calculatorsEach with its formula and limits

Quick answers to the arithmetic that comes up in every SI and PI design. Each calculator shows its inputs, its numbers and a live chart together, states the formula and its assumptions, and keeps the calculation in its link so you can share exactly what you saw.

Every result is checked against something the code does not compute itself — an analytical limit, a published value or an independently derived formula — and the page for each one says what it assumes. For the models behind the site’s instruments, see model assumptions; for longer explorations, the labs.
