Explore one channel comparison

Three channels, all 8 inches, all 16 GT/s, all with the loss budget set to 14 dB. One has an impedance step, one has no added discontinuity, and one has a 28 ps via stub. The openings are 254 mV, 253 mV and 48 mV, although all three begin with the same loss-budget setting.

Compare the modeled channel responses to see why the eye openings differ. The first two really are matched: 14.06 dB and 14.00 dB at Nyquist, and their eyes agree to 0.7%. The third is 24.15 dB — ten decibels more — because a 28 ps stub is not extra attenuation, it is a quarter-wave notch at 8.93 GHz, close to the 8 GHz Nyquist frequency. The comparison shows why a loss budget alone does not describe the complete channel response.

To explore the comparison, open Lab B, press PCIe Gen 4, then …no step at all, then …same budget, a stub, and watch the IL at Nyquist readout rather than the loss slider.
